💡 How to Hook this into Radarr

Since Radarr doesn't support raw CSV upload directly, follow these simple and popular methods to auto-import your lists:

  1. Export your selected movies using the **💾 EXPORT CSV** button.
  2. Go to Trakt.tv, create/open a custom list.
  3. Click **Import** and select the exported CSV file.
  4. In Radarr, navigate to **Settings > Import Lists > + (Add) > Trakt List**.
  5. Connect your Trakt account and select your custom list. Radarr will now automatically sync it!

  3. Upload your exported CSV file.
  4. Copy the generated custom list RSS/URL from MDBList.
  5. In Radarr, go to **Settings > Import Lists > + (Add) > Custom List** and paste the MDBList URL.

  4. Enter your TMDb username and TMDb List ID. Radarr will sync the list directly!
